Chronic & acute kidney disease

Staging, slowing progression and managing complications of chronic kidney disease (CKD), plus urgent assessment of acute kidney injury (AKI) after infection, dehydration or medicines.

  • CKD stages 1–5
  • Acute kidney injury
  • Rising creatinine
  • Protein in urine
  • Anaemia of CKD
{{icon:gauge}}

Diabetes, blood pressure & the kidney

Evaluation of essential, resistant and secondary hypertension, including suspected renal artery narrowing. Care for diabetic nephropathy and cardiorenal concerns brings blood pressure, diabetes and kidney function into the same review.

  • Diabetic kidney disease
  • Primary & secondary hypertension
  • Resistant hypertension
  • Renal artery stenosis evaluation
  • Cardiorenal syndrome
{{icon:flask}}

Glomerular & autoimmune kidney disease

Assessment of protein or blood in urine, glomerulonephritis, nephrotic and nephritic syndromes, lupus nephritis and vasculitis. Investigations and treatment are tailored to the diagnosis.

  • Glomerulonephritis
  • Nephrotic syndrome
  • Lupus nephritis
  • Vasculitis
  • Proteinuria
  • Haematuria
{{icon:drop}}

Electrolyte, acid–base & stone disorders

Assessment of sodium, potassium, calcium, fluid and acid–base imbalances. Recurrent kidney stones can be reviewed with a metabolic work-up and a medical prevention plan; surgical stone treatment is a separate urology service.

  • Sodium & potassium imbalance
  • Kidney stones (medical)
  • Recurrent UTI
  • Calcium & fluid balance
  • Metabolic stone work-up
{{icon:dialysis}}

Dialysis planning & care

Outpatient haemodialysis coordination, vascular access planning and AV fistula/graft surveillance. Peritoneal dialysis care includes CAPD/APD education, training coordination and follow-up.

  • Haemodialysis
  • Haemodiafiltration
  • CAPD & APD
  • Hospital-based dialysis coordination
  • Home dialysis guidance
  • AV fistula & graft review
{{icon:transplant}}

Kidney transplant work-up & follow-up

Recipient and donor evaluation, counselling for families, and lifelong post-transplant care including immunosuppression monitoring.

  • Living donor
  • Deceased donor
  • ABO-incompatible
  • Swap transplant
  • Post-transplant care
{{icon:baby}}

Children & pregnancy

Evaluation of kidney problems in children and pregnancy-related kidney injury.

  • Paediatric kidney disease
  • Congenital anomalies
  • Pregnancy-related AKI
{{icon:needle}}

Interventional nephrology

Assessment and planning for hospital-based procedures, with the appropriate setting discussed before treatment.

  • Image-guided kidney biopsy
  • Dialysis catheter insertion
  • PD catheter insertion
  • AV fistula cannulation
  • Plasmapheresis
{{icon:book}}

Renal nutrition & prevention

Personalised counselling on sodium, protein, potassium and phosphorus, guided by kidney stage, medicines and blood results. Your plan can also address fluid intake, diabetes and recurrent stones.

  • Renal diet review
  • Diabetes nutrition
  • Stone prevention
  • Fluid planning
{{icon:lab}}

Kidney health check

A doctor-led review of kidney function and urine results, particularly for people with diabetes, hypertension or a family history of kidney disease. Testing is selected for your symptoms and risk factors.

  • Serum creatinine & eGFR
  • Urine ACR
  • Urine routine & microscopy
  • Spot urine protein-to-creatinine ratio
  • Electrolytes
  • Ultrasound KUB
  • ABPM assessment & report review

When to see a nephrologist

  • Creatinine above normal or eGFR below 60
  • Protein or blood in urine
  • Swelling of feet, face or eyelids
  • BP that stays high despite medicines
  • Diabetes for more than 5 years
