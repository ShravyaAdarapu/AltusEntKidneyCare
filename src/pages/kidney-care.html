Chronic & acute kidney disease

Staging, slowing progression and managing complications of chronic kidney disease (CKD), plus urgent assessment of acute kidney injury (AKI) after infection, dehydration or medicines.

  • CKD stages 1–5
  • Acute kidney injury
  • Rising creatinine
  • Protein in urine
  • Anaemia of CKD
{{icon:gauge}}

Diabetes, blood pressure & the kidney

Diabetes and high blood pressure cause most kidney failure in India. With a fellowship in diabetes, Dr. Chetan manages sugar, BP and kidney protection together in one plan.

  • Diabetic kidney disease
  • Primary & secondary hypertension
  • Resistant hypertension
{{icon:flask}}

Glomerular & autoimmune kidney disease

Diagnosis and immunosuppressive treatment for nephrotic and nephritic syndromes, lupus nephritis and vasculitis.

  • Glomerulonephritis
  • Nephrotic syndrome
  • Lupus nephritis
  • Vasculitis
{{icon:drop}}

Electrolyte, acid–base & stone disorders

Low sodium or potassium, metabolic acidosis, recurring kidney stones and urinary infections.

  • Sodium & potassium imbalance
  • Kidney stones (medical)
  • Recurrent UTI

Dialysis planning & care

Timely access planning, starting dialysis safely and long-term follow-up, including home-based peritoneal dialysis for patients who prefer it.

  • Haemodialysis
  • Haemodiafiltration
  • CAPD & APD
  • CRRT (critical care)
  • Home dialysis guidance

Kidney transplant work-up & follow-up

Recipient and donor evaluation, counselling for families, and lifelong post-transplant care including immunosuppression monitoring.

  • Living donor
  • Deceased donor
  • ABO-incompatible
  • Swap transplant
  • Post-transplant care

  • Pregnancy-related AKI

When to see a nephrologist

  • Creatinine above normal or eGFR below 60
  • Protein or blood in urine
  • Swelling of feet, face or eyelids
  • BP that stays high despite medicines
  • Diabetes for more than 5 years
