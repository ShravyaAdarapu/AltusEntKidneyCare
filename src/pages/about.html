About Altus

Specialist care, closer to home

Altus ENT & Kidney Care is a new specialist clinic in Shaikpet, started by a nephrologist and an ENT surgeon who wanted patients to have more time with their doctor.

Our story

Why kidney and ENT, together?

Dr. Chetan Veeramaneni and Dr. T. Mounika have spent their careers in large teaching and corporate hospitals in Hyderabad. They saw the same thing every day: patients waiting hours for a few minutes of specialist time, then struggling to get follow-up.

Altus is their answer: a calm clinic with timed appointments and evening hours, where the specialist who sees you also follows you up. Kidney and ENT care sit together naturally. Many patients with diabetes, high blood pressure or long-term medicines need both, and families can now see both doctors in one place.

Altus means "high" or "deep" in Latin. For us, it stands for high standards of care.
