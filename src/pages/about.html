About Altus

An ENT and kidney care clinic in Shaikpet, owned and run by Dr. Chetan Veeramaneni and Dr. T. Mounika Rao.

Who you’ll meet

Kidney care

Dr. Chetan Kumar Veeramaneni

MBBS · DNB General Medicine · DM Nephrology

Dr. Chetan sees people with acute and chronic kidney disease, kidney concerns related to diabetes or blood pressure, and those preparing for dialysis or a transplant. He also provides transplant follow-up.

Nephrology training at Osmania Medical College & General Hospital, with a certificate in kidney transplant management from Mayo Clinic.

ENT care

Dr. T. Mounika Rao

MBBS · MS ENT · Fellowship in Advanced Rhinology & Skull Base Surgery

Dr. Mounika sees adults and children with hearing, sinus, throat and voice concerns. Her work includes endoscopic ear and nasal procedures, as well as advanced rhinology and skull base surgery.

MS in ENT from Mamata Medical College, followed by fellowship training at Royal Pearl Hospital, Trichy.
