Nose & sinus

Blocked nose, sinus headaches, nasal polyps, allergies and nosebleeds, treated with medicines first and minimally invasive endoscopic surgery when needed.

  • Chronic sinusitis
  • Nasal polyps
  • Deviated septum
  • Allergic rhinitis
  • Nosebleeds
  • Loss of smell

Surgery: Functional endoscopic sinus surgery (FESS), endoscopic septoplasty, polypectomy, nasal bone fracture reduction.

{{icon:hearing}}

Ear & hearing

Ear pain, discharge, perforated eardrum, hearing loss, ringing and dizziness, with microscopic and endoscopic ear surgery.

  • Ear infections (CSOM)
  • Perforated eardrum
  • Hearing loss
  • Otosclerosis
  • Tinnitus
  • Vertigo

Surgery: Tympanoplasty, stapedotomy, mastoidectomy, myringotomy with grommets, intratympanic steroid injection, earlobe repair.

{{icon:voice}}

Throat & voice

Recurrent sore throat, tonsillitis, hoarseness, swallowing difficulty and snoring.

  • Tonsillitis
  • Adenoids in children
  • Hoarse voice
  • Vocal cord lesions
  • Snoring

Surgery: Adenotonsillectomy, microlaryngeal surgery, oesophagoscopy and bronchoscopy for adults and children.

{{icon:thyroid}}

Head & neck

Neck lumps, thyroid swellings, salivary gland problems and airway care.

  • Thyroid nodules & goitre
  • Neck swellings
  • Lipoma & cysts
  • Preauricular sinus

Surgery: Total and hemi-thyroidectomy, excision biopsies, tracheostomy.

{{icon:scope}}

Advanced rhinology & skull base

Dr. Mounika completed a fellowship in Advanced Rhinology & Skull Base Surgery. These endoscopic procedures through the nose avoid external cuts.

  • CSF rhinorrhoea repair
  • Optic nerve decompression
  • Medial orbitotomy
  • Pituitary access (with neurosurgery)

When to see an ENT

  • Blocked nose for more than 12 weeks
  • Ear discharge or sudden hearing loss
  • Hoarse voice for more than 3 weeks
  • A neck lump that doesn't go away
  • Snoring with pauses in breathing
