About

Kidney care that looks at the whole patient

Dr. Chetan Kumar Veeramaneni is a consultant nephrologist and kidney transplant physician. His clinical work includes acute and chronic kidney disease, dialysis, and transplant assessment and follow-up. He also evaluates kidney complications related to diabetes and high blood pressure.
