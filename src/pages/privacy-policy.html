Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

When you book through this website, you share your name, phone number, age, preferred date and a short description of your concern. Appointment requests and enquiries are stored securely for the clinic’s authorised staff to arrange your visit and respond to you. Please share only the information needed for your request. Contact the clinic to request access, corrections or deletion, subject to applicable record-keeping requirements.

How we use it

Only to schedule your appointment, contact you about your visit and keep your medical record at the clinic. We never sell or share your information for marketing.

Medical records

Clinical records are kept confidential under applicable Indian law and professional guidelines, and are shared only with you, people you authorise, or as required by law.

Website analytics

We may use privacy-friendly analytics to understand how visitors use the site. These do not identify you personally.

Medical disclaimer

Content on this website is for general information and does not replace a consultation with a qualified doctor.
